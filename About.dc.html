About us

Three decades of honest honey from Kerala

Indian Honey Park began in 1990 with a handful of hives and a simple rule we still follow: bottle the honey exactly as the bees made it.

Our story

From family apiary to India's kitchens

What started as a small beekeeping unit in Kerala now works with more than 300 partner beekeepers across the Western Ghats, Kashmir and the Nilgiris. We buy directly, pay above market rate, and never blend batches to hit a price point.

Our fruits-in-honey range came out of a home experiment — whole rambutan resting in a jar of wild honey. It is now what most customers come back for.
