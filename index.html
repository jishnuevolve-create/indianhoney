Why choose us

Why Choose Our Products

Single-origin sourcing

Every batch is traced back to the apiary it came from — Kashmir, the Western Ghats, or our own farms.

Cold-extracted, never heated

Enzymes and pollen stay intact because we never pasteurise or filter the goodness out.

Real fruit inside

Kiwi, plum, amla, rambutan and pomelo, preserved whole in honey — no syrups, no colours.

Lab tested every batch

Purity, moisture and antibiotic residue reports for each lot, available on request.
